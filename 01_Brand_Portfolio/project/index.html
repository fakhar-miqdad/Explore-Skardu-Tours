About Us

Your Local Gateway to the Heart of the Karakoram

Explore Skardu Tours is a locally owned and operated travel company based in Skardu, Gilgit-Baltistan. We were born from a deep love for these mountains, lakes, and valleys, and a desire to share them with the world in a way that is authentic, responsible, and unforgettable.

From the turquoise waters of Shangrila to the wild plains of Deosai, our team crafts journeys that go beyond sightseeing, connecting you with the culture, hospitality, and breathtaking landscapes that make Skardu truly extraordinary.

Rooted in Skardu. Inspired by nature.

Destinations

Iconic Places of Skardu

Four unforgettable destinations that capture the soul of Gilgit-Baltistan, from mirror-still lakes to the last Himalayan kingdom fort.

Lakeside Resort

Shangrila Lake

Known as the "Heaven on Earth," this lower Kachura lake features turquoise waters, blooming gardens, and the iconic red-roofed Shangrila Resort set against towering peaks.

Hidden Gem

Upper Kachura Lake

A serene, less-explored lake with crystal-clear waters reflecting the surrounding Karakoram summity. The tranquil setting is perfect for boating and quiet contemplation.

Heritage Fort

Shigar Fort

A 17th-century palace-fort of the Raja of Shigar, beautifully restored from local stone and wood. Wander centuries of Balti history amid the dramatic Shigar valley.

National Park

Deosai National Park

The "Land of Giants," a high-altitude plateau at 4,100 metres carpeted in wildflowers each summer. Home to the Himalayan brown bear, and some of the purest night skies on Earth.
